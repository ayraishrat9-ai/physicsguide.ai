Famous Physicists

Albert Einstein

Relativity theory (special & general), E=mc², photoelectric effect. Nobel Prize 1921.

Isaac Newton

Laws of motion and universal gravitation, calculus, reflecting telescope.

Niels Bohr

Bohr model of atom, quantum mechanics, Copenhagen interpretation.

Max Planck

Founder of quantum theory, Planck's quantum hypothesis.

Richard Feynman

Quantum electrodynamics (QED), Feynman diagrams, quantum computing.

Stephen Hawking

Black hole radiation (Hawking radiation), singularity theorems.

Marie Curie

Radioactivity research, discovered polonium and radium.

Erwin Schrödinger

Schrödinger equation, wave mechanics, quantum superposition.

Werner Heisenberg

Uncertainty principle, matrix mechanics, quantum mechanics.

Muslim Scientists in Physics

Ibn al-Haytham (Alhazen)

Father of modern optics, camera obscura, scientific method. Book of Optics.

Al-Biruni

Calculated Earth's circumference, studied specific gravity, astronomical observations.

Ibn Sina (Avicenna)

Canon of Medicine, physics concepts of motion and time.

Al-Khwarizmi

Founder of algebra (algorithm named after him), Hindu numerals.

Al-Farabi

Physics of music, vacuum theory, "Second Teacher" after Aristotle.

Ibn Rushd (Averroes)

Aristotle commentaries, physics of motion.

Al-Tusi

Tusi couple (planetary motion geometry), trigonometry as separate field.

Ibn al-Shatir

Heliocentric model before Copernicus, astronomical instruments.

Al-Kindi

Cryptanalysis pioneer, frequency analysis for code-breaking.
